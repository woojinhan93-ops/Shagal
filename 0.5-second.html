01 — The Mechanism

Familiar. Then slightly wrong. Then it clicks.

The strongest expressions don't land immediately — they open a tiny gap between recognition and reinterpretation, and the current moment rushes in to close it.

FamiliarityYou've seen this word
→
StrangenessSomething's off
→
ReinterpretationWait —
→
RecognitionThat's exactly it
→
Emotional impact어? 아... 와.
“How did nobody say this before?” The reaction this is built to find — not laughter, necessarily. Recognition, catharsis, anger, tenderness, shock. Whatever moves.

02 — Calibration

Benchmarks, not formulas.

Every candidate is scored against expressions that already worked — not to copy their words, but to borrow the structure underneath them.

매국노→애국노
9.5/10

A powerful original, one syllable reversed, the emotional direction fully flipped. The current controversy does the rest.

장애 + 특혜→장애특혜
8.7/10

Two words from opposing emotional territory, compressed into a phrase that shouldn't sit together — and does.

조선 + 롤스로이스→조선 롤스로이스
8.5–8.8

A familiar local identity dragged into a disproportionately prestigious category. Absurd elevation, instantly readable.

03 — Generators

Eight ways in, one way out.

No mechanism is forced onto every issue. Each candidate issue runs through several of these in parallel before anything gets compared.

A

Minimal Mutation

Take an already powerful expression and change as little of it as possible.

B

Semantic Reversal

Invert the emotional or conceptual direction of a familiar word.

C

Semantic Diagonal

Pair two concepts from spaces that normally never touch.

D

Identity Collision

Expected identity meets the behavior actually drawing attention.

E

Status Collision

Something ordinary, suddenly promoted into a prestigious category.

F

Double Reading

A phonetic, morphological, or bilingual second meaning hiding in plain sight.

G

Existing Meme Mining

Find what the internet already accidentally named, before inventing anything.

H

Cultural Translation

Move the issue whole into a completely different semantic field.

04 — The Standard

One exceptional result.
Not three good ones.

A dozen candidates get generated for every one that survives. Presenting the mediocre ones just to look productive breaks the entire premise.

“이번 판은 아직 9점짜리를 못 찾았다.” “No 9-point candidate this round.” — said on purpose, not as a failure.
